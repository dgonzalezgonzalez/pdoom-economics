The ties robustness assigns half outcomes to ties and drops both-bad responses. It fits a fractional-outcome logit as a descriptive comparison rather than interpreting both-bad responses as indifference between acceptable models. The all-rounds specification admits repeated votes and receives less evidential weight. The GPQA exercise matches explicit benchmark model versions where available; its ECI counterpart uses identical comparisons. ECI and GPQA need not share evaluation budgets.

The crosswalk records unmatched and qualified entries rather than imputing their capability. Standard errors do not resolve mapping uncertainty. Nor does the analysis hold response style, speed, token use, or topic difficulty fixed. Identifying willingness to pay requires monetary choices or price variation with a demand model. Identifying developer profits further requires quantities, inference costs, research costs, financing, and complementary-product revenues.

The support solver verifies that supported actions are best responses and excluded actions do not improve payoffs. Linear feasibility programs accommodate unequal supports and degenerate games. Probability recursions are undiscounted; payoff recursions are discounted. Keeping them separate prevents interpreting discounted hazards as extinction probabilities.

The no-delegation certificate is a theoretical characterization. The numerical solver searches unrestricted stage games rather than presuming it holds. Multiple-pure diagnostics count continuation state games, not all complete-game equilibria. Checks cover off-path states because subgame perfection requires optimality there too. Initial-state boundary diagnostics isolate whether a computational limit affects the reported trajectory; expanded-domain and mesh comparisons assess numerical stability separately.

\section{Reproducibility and transparency}
The repository contains LaTeX, proofs, references, frozen numeric data, a reviewed crosswalk, SHA-256 manifests, Python solvers and estimators, generated figures and tables, and validation outputs. Copyrighted reference-paper PDFs remain in an ignored local cache and are not redistributed. Arena data are attributed under CC-BY-4.0; Epoch and benchmark sources retain their original attribution and licensing terms.

The command \texttt{python replication/run.py} verifies frozen inputs, runs behavioral and accounting checks, reproduces estimates and scenarios, writes manuscript tables, compiles the paper, and renders visual-inspection copies. Normal replication uses frozen data rather than querying a changing leaderboard.

This working paper was developed with substantial AI assistance in formalization, programming, literature checking, and writing. One external ChatGPT referee pass is requested for this frozen draft; its completed report and a point-by-point response will accompany the revision. That review is an additional consistency check, not independent academic peer review. Mathematical claims and computational limitations remain open to human scrutiny.
